# Snatchd Privacy Policy
_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Snatchd ("the app") is made by Riley Luke Smith (the App Store seller name), trading as Ashforge ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects and why. Short version: **your photos never leave your phone, and we don't know who you are.**

## Your photos
- Reference photos you pick, paste or share into Snatchd, and the photos you take with it, are processed **on your device** using Apple's Vision framework. They are **not uploaded** to us or anyone else.
- They're stored only inside the app on your phone, until you delete them in the app or delete the app.
- When you share a **link** (for example a post from another app) into Snatchd, your phone downloads the preview image from that link directly from the website that hosts it, the same way a browser would. We don't receive the link or the image.
- The camera is used only while the camera screen is open, to show the pose outline and take your photo. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

## What we collect
We don't ask for your name, email or any account. The app creates a random ID on your device (not linked to you, your Apple ID or your device ID) and uses it for:

| Data | Why | Service |
|---|---|---|
| Random app ID + how the app is used (e.g. "opened camera", "match score 87%", "paywall shown") | To understand which features work and fix the ones that don't | PostHog (analytics, USA) |
| Crash reports and diagnostics (device model, iOS version, the error) | To fix crashes | Sentry (USA) |
| Purchase status (whether Snatchd Pro is active) | To unlock Snatchd Pro and restore purchases | RevenueCat (USA), Apple |

None of this includes your photos, your name, contacts, location, or anything that identifies you. We don't use it for advertising, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share it with anyone except the service providers above, who process it on our behalf.

## Payments
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt from Apple so the app knows Snatchd Pro is active.

## Keeping and deleting data
- Everything on your phone (photos, poses, shots) is deleted when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
- Analytics and crash data are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted. Because they're tied only to a random ID, we can't match them to you. If you want them deleted anyway, email us and we'll explain how to find your ID.

## Children
Snatchd isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, Australia or California) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we hold no information that identifies you, there's usually nothing to give back, but email us and we'll help.

## Changes
If this policy changes we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
