# Snatchd support
Snatchd lays the outline of any photo's pose over your camera and tells whoever's holding the phone what to fix.

**Help or feedback:** email [ashforge.apps@gmail.com](mailto:ashforge.apps@gmail.com) and we'll reply within 2 business days.

**Subscriptions:** manage or cancel Snatchd Pro in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Restore a purchase in the app: Me → Restore purchases.
